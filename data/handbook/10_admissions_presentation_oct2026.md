## 1. University Snapshot & Rankings

* **Tagline**: "Step into the next-gen living-learning campus." SR University describes itself as India's youngest university to achieve national and global rankings and accreditation, and as one of India's youngest and fastest-growing universities.
* **Campus**: 170+ acre lush green sprawling campus at Warangal, Telangana.
* **NIRF (Engineering category)**: Rank **98** in 2023, **98** in 2024 and **91** in 2025. Top 100 engineering institutions in India for four years in a row. The presentation also describes SRU as #1 in Telangana.
* **Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2026**:
  - Overall: global band **801–1000**; **7th** among private universities in India.
  - Asia rankings 2026: rank band **351–400**; **7th** among Indian private universities.
  - Computer Science: global band 601–800; national rank **21**.
  - Engineering: global band 601–800; national rank **18**.
  - University category: global band 801–1000; national rank **28**.
  - Research Quality: **6th** in India (global band 801–1000).
* **THE Sustainability Impact Ratings 2026 (overall)**: **24th** in India; global band **401–600**.
* **UI GreenMetric World University Rankings 2025**: **23rd** in India.
* **ICAR Accreditation**: for both the School of Agriculture and the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programme; SRU is the first private university in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh to achieve it.
* **NBA**: Tier-I accreditation for UG engineering programmes.

## 3. International Programs & Pathways

* **International Engineering Program (IEP)** (the presentation shows an ABET accreditation mark): in collaboration with US universities, with an M.S. in the 5th year.
  - **3+1**: Years 1–3 at SR University, Year 4 at a partner university in the USA.
  - **2+2**: Years 1–2 at SR University, Years 3–4 at a partner university in the USA.
  - Partner universities: University of Massachusetts Lowell, University of New Haven, University of Missouri (Mizzou), University of Central Missouri. Terms & conditions apply.
  - 43 students have transferred to US partner universities under the IEP (as shown in the presentation).
* **International Business Accelerated Program (IBAP)**: BBA + MBA/MS (Finance / Business Analytics); **3+2** = 3 years at SR University + 2 years at UMass Lowell, USA.
* **2+2 with New Zealand**: Years 1–2 at SR University; Years 3–4 in New Zealand. B.Sc. in Food Science at the University of Otago; B.Sc. in Agriculture at Massey University.
* **Accelerated Masters Programs (progression pathways)**:
  - Louisiana State University – Shreveport (LSUS), USA: M.S. in Computer Systems Technology — 3.5 years at SRU + 1.5 years at LSUS.
  - University of Missouri Kansas City (UMKC), Business (Management): M.S. in Finance, Public Administration and Professional Master of Business Administration (PMBA) — 2.5 years at SRU + 1.5 years at UMKC.
  - UMKC, STEM: M.S. in Computer Science, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering — 3.5 years at SRU + 1.5 years at UMKC.
  - University of Florida (UF): M.S. in Computer Science — 3.5 years at SRU + 1.5 years at UF.
  - Research collaboration with Missouri University of Science and Technology (Missouri S&T).
* **Semester Abroad (2025)**: students studied a semester at partner institutions including the University of Florida, FPT University and UCSI University.
* **International students**: students from more than 15 countries study at SR University. Students come from across India, with the largest representation from Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
