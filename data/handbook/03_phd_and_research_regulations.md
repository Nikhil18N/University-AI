# SR University — Ph.D. Regulations & Research Policies

## 1. Ph.D. Categories & Admission Modes
* **Full-Time Scholar**: Scholars pursuing full-time research on campus at SR University.
* **Part-Time Scholar**: Candidates employed in recognized universities, colleges, research institutions, or industries, who submit a valid No Objection Certificate (NOC) confirming relief for coursework.
* **Conversion**: Full-time to part-time or vice-versa is permitted upon recommendation of the Scholar Research Committee (SRC) and approval of the Dean (Research).

---

## 2. Competitive Ph.D. Assistantships / Fellowships
SR University provides competitive monthly financial assistantships for full-time research scholars:
* **₹50,000 per month**: For full-time scholars whose immediately preceding degree is from premier institutes (**IITs, NITs, or IIITs**).
* **₹40,000 per month**: For all other eligible full-time scholars.
* **₹25,000 per month + Free Campus Accommodation & Meals**: For full-time scholars who choose to stay in university hostels. Includes room, three meals daily, snacks, and laundry facility without any additional charges.
* **Tenure**: Valid for a period of **3 years** from the date of admission registration (extendable up to two times for 6 months in exceptional cases).
* **Assistantship Conditions**:
  - Scholars must provide **8 hours per week** of teaching/laboratory assistance.
  - Must maintain a student teaching feedback score of **$\ge 4.0$ out of 5.0**.
  - Must submit monthly progress report and claim form through the `Sruniv`/`SRAAP` web portal by the 25th of every month.

---

## 3. Ph.D. Eligibility Criteria
* **Master's Degree Pathway**: Master's degree (1-year/2-semester after 4-year B.Tech, or 2-year/4-semester after 3-year degree) with at least **55% marks in aggregate** or equivalent CGPA.
* **Direct After 4-Year B.Tech Pathway**: A 4-year / 8-semester bachelor's degree with a minimum of **75% marks in aggregate** (or equivalent grade point). Direct B.Tech entrants must take an additional 12 credits of coursework.
* **M.Phil Pathway**: M.Phil with at least **55% marks in aggregate**.
* **Relaxation**: A **5% relaxation** (50% qualifying marks) is allowed for candidates belonging to SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer), Differently-Abled, and Economically Weaker Section (EWS) categories.

---

## 4. Selection Process & Weightage
* **Application**: Online via `https://admission.sru.edu.in/`.
* **Entrance Test Exemption**: Candidates with valid qualification in **UGC-NET, UGC-CSIR NET, GATE, CEED, or SET** are exempt from the written entrance exam.
* **SRU Written Eligibility Test**: For other candidates; consists of 50% Research Methodology and 50% subject-specific syllabus.
* **Selection Weightage**:
  - **70% Weightage**: Performance in SRU Eligibility Test / National Qualifying Exam.
  - **30% Weightage**: Interview and research proposal presentation before the Department Selection Committee (DSC).

---

## 5. Coursework & Comprehensive Examination
* **Coursework Requirement**: Minimum of **12 credits** (including a mandatory 4-credit course on Research Methodology, Research & Publication Ethics). Direct B.Tech entrants require 24 credits.
* **Performance Benchmark**: Must secure a minimum of **60% marks in each course** and an overall **CGPA $\ge 7.0$** on a 10-point scale within 2 semesters (maximum 2 years with extension).
* **Comprehensive Examination**: Oral examination conducted by SRC and external experts after coursework completion.
  - Satisfactory -> Ph.D. registration confirmed.
  - Unsatisfactory -> 1 repeat attempt allowed within 45 days. If failed again, registration is cancelled.

---

## 6. Synopsis & Thesis Submission Criteria
* **Synopsis Prerequisites**:
  - Publication of at least **one Q1 SCIE journal paper** as first author with SR University affiliation: `"SR University, Warangal, Telangana - 506371"`.
  - Equivalencies: 1 Q1 SCIE paper = 2 non-Q1 SCIE papers = 2 Scopus indexed journal papers = 4 Scopus conference papers.
  - Plagiarism check through university Turnitin software.
* **Thesis Evaluation**:
  - Prepared within 90 days of synopsis acceptance.
  - Minimum 3 years, maximum 6 years from date of admission (relaxation up to 2 years for female scholars and PwD).
  - Adjudicated by supervisor and at least two external examiners from a panel of 10 experts nominated by the Vice-Chancellor.
  - Public Viva-Voce defense examination.

---

## 7. Anti-Plagiarism Policy & Penalties (Turnitin)
All academic documents, conference papers, journal papers, and Ph.D. theses are verified using Turnitin / iThenticate.
* **Level 0 (Similarity up to 10%)**: No penalty; passed as per regulations.
* **Level 1 (Similarity 10% to 40%)**: Scholar is instructed to revise and resubmit within 30 days.
* **Level 2 (Similarity 40% to 60%)**: Debarred from submitting revised thesis for 1 year; show-cause notice issued.
* **Level 3 (Similarity > 60%)**: Registration cancelled; degree/credit revoked; disciplinary action initiated against supervisor.

---

## 8. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) & Revenue Sharing
* **Policy**: Encourages commercialization and patent filing for inventions created using university resources.
* **Affiliation**: All patent applications and IPR filings must bear the university address: `"SR University, Warangal, Telangana - 506371"`.
* **Revenue Distribution**: Commercialization profits and licensing revenues from spin-offs are shared equitably between the researcher/inventor and SR University under the guidelines of the IPR Policy Committee chaired by the Registrar.
