# SR University — Admissions, Eligibility, Fee Structure & Scholarships (Official 2026-27 / 2027-28)

## 1. Undergraduate (B.Tech) Admissions & Eligibility Criteria
* **General B.Tech Eligibility**:
  - Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech programs at SR University must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate or an equivalent qualifying examination with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks** in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) or relevant subjects.
  - The cutoff is strictly **50%**, NOT 60%. A student with 58% or 52% in Intermediate is **fully eligible** for B.Tech admission.
  - For state boards like Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TS BIE) / Andhra Pradesh BIE where total maximum marks are 1000, 50% corresponds to **500 marks out of 1000**.
  - Borderline applicants or students uncertain about their marks are never turned away; they are encouraged to contact the admissions office directly for counseling.
* **Eligible Programs for BiPC / Non-MPC Students**:
  - Students with 10+2 in Biology, Physics, and Chemistry (BiPC) are eligible for programs such as **B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture**, **Biotechnology**, and allied health/science programs.
* **Undergraduate Management (BBA)**:
  - Candidates must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate (any stream) with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks**.
* **Postgraduate Programs (MBA / M.Tech)**:
  - Candidates seeking MBA admission must possess a recognized bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks**.
  - PG students are eligible for an additional **10% scholarship**.
* **Entrance Exams & Rank Assessment**:
  - Admissions and merit scholarships consider national and state entrance tests including **JEE Main**, **TG EAPCET / EAMCET**, **SRUEE (SR University Entrance Exam)**, **SAT**, and **Pearson Undergraduate Test**.
  - Ranks and scores directly determine scholarship eligibility slabs.

## 2. Official Fee Structure (Academic Year 2026-27 / 2027-28)
* **Tuition Fee Schedule (Semester-Wise)**:
  - SR University tuition fees are officially listed and collected on a **per-semester basis** (2 semesters per academic year).
* **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**:
  - **Tuition Fee**: **₹1,47,500 per semester** (equals **₹2,95,000 per year**).
  - **One-time Fees (Year 1 only)**: Enrollment fee of **₹5,000** + Admission fee of **₹25,000** = ₹30,000 one-time.
  - Total first-year investment (excluding hostel): ₹2,95,000 (tuition) + ₹30,000 (one-time) = **₹3,25,000**.
* **B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML)**:
  - **Tuition Fee**: **₹1,47,500 per semester** (**₹2,95,000 per year**).
  - **Specialization Fee**: **₹30,000 per year** starting from **Year 2 onwards**.
  - One-time Fees (Year 1): **₹5,000** enrollment fee + **₹25,000** admission fee.
* **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**:
  - **Tuition Fee**: **₹1,00,000 per semester** (equals **₹2,00,000 per year**).
* **Other Engineering Branches (ECE, EEE, Civil)**:
  - Tuition Fee: **₹1,00,000 to ₹1,25,000 per semester** (**₹2,00,000 to ₹2,50,000 per year**).
* **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)**:
  - **Tuition Fee**: **₹90,000 per semester** (equals **₹1,80,000 per year**).
* **MBA (Master of Business Administration)**:
  - **Tuition Fee**: **₹1,50,000 per semester** (equals **₹3,00,000 per year**).
  - All eligible PG students receive an additional **10% scholarship**.

## 4. Official Merit Scholarships & Concessions
* **Merit Scholarship Basis**:
  - Awarded strictly on academic excellence in 10+2 / Intermediate marks or national/state entrance exam ranks (JEE Main / TG EAPCET / SAT).
* **Merit Scholarship Slabs (B.Tech Tuition Fee Waivers)**:
  - **98% and Above (or top rankers)**: **50% to 100% Tuition Fee Scholarship**.
  - **92% to 97.99% in Intermediate**: **40% Tuition Fee Scholarship for CSE**!
    - *Example Calculation*: For a student scoring 92% in Intermediate joining B.Tech CSE, the 40% scholarship reduces the per-semester tuition from ₹1,47,500 down to **₹88,500 per semester** (total annual tuition of **₹1,77,000** instead of ₹2,95,000).
  - **85% to 91.99% in Intermediate**: **25% Tuition Fee Scholarship**.
  - **75% to 84.99% in Intermediate**: **15% Tuition Fee Scholarship**.
* **Postgraduate Special Scholarship**:
  - All admitted MBA and M.Tech postgraduate students qualify for an additional **10% tuition scholarship**.
* **Application Guideline**:
  - Scholarships are allocated on a merit basis during the admissions cycle. Students should apply early at `https://apply.sru.edu.in` to secure their eligible scholarship slab.
