# SR University — Admissions, Eligibility, Fee Structure & Scholarships

## 1. Undergraduate (B.Tech) Admissions & Eligibility Criteria
* **General Eligibility**: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech programs at SR University must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate or an equivalent qualifying examination with a minimum of **60% aggregate marks** in required subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry / Computer Science).
* **Mark Calculation & Total Marks**:
  - The standard total maximum marks for 12th class / Intermediate is **1000 marks** (as prevalent in state boards like Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education / Andhra Pradesh BIE).
  - Minimum cutoff of 60% corresponds to **600 marks out of 1000**.
  - If a student scores below 600 (e.g., 599 marks out of 1000 = 59.9%), the score is below the mandatory 60% cutoff. Such borderline applicants must contact the admissions office directly for special evaluation.
  - For CBSE, ICSE, or other state boards where the total marks differ (e.g. 500 total marks), eligibility is calculated proportionally using the exact percentage: $\frac{\text{Obtained Marks}}{\text{Total Marks}} \times 100 \ge 60\%$.
* **Applicability Across Branches**:
  - The 60% minimum eligibility requirement applies uniformly across **all engineering branches**, including Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI&ML), Data Science, Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE), and Mechanical Engineering (ME).
* **Direct & Management Quota Admissions**:
  - Direct / management quota admissions are offered for eligible candidates across all branches.
  - **No Entrance Exam Required**: Students applying under direct / management quota admission do **NOT** need to appear for or submit scores from any entrance exam (such as JEE Main, TS EAMCET, or SRU written test). Admission is granted based purely on 12th / Intermediate board marks and seat availability.
* **Admissions Contact**:
  - Admissions Helpline: **833 100 3030** / **833 100 4040**
  - Admissions Office Email: `admissions@sru.edu.in`
  - Portal: `https://sru.edu.in/admissions`

## 2. Fee Structure & Payment Guidelines
* **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**:
  - **Annual Tuition Fee**: **Rs. 2,10,000/-** per year (plus other standard university charges).
* **Other Engineering & Degree Programs**:
  - The annual tuition fee for other B.Tech branches (ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil) and other programs is typically **Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 30,000 lower** than the CSE fee structure (e.g., approx Rs. 1,80,000 to Rs. 1,90,000 per year).
* **Payment Schedule (Semester-wise)**:
  - Tuition fees must be paid on a **semester basis**.
  - Each semester, students pay **half of the annual tuition fee** along with other applicable semester fees.
  - For CSE: **Rs. 1,05,000/- per semester** (plus applicable university/exam fees).
* **Hostel & Transport Fees**:
  - Paid separately per semester/year depending on room type (AC / Non-AC) and bus route.

## 3. Merit Scholarships & Concessions (B.Tech & Undergraduate)
* **Eligibility Basis**:
  - Merit scholarships at SR University are awarded strictly based on **12th / Intermediate marks**.
* **Merit Scholarship Slabs**:
  - **970 Marks and Above out of 1000 (97%+ marks)**: Eligible for **up to 30% tuition fee scholarship**.
  - **900 to 969 Marks out of 1000 (90% to 96.9% marks)**: Eligible for **up to 20% tuition fee scholarship** (e.g., a student with 920 marks qualifies for up to 20% scholarship).
* **Allocation Policy**:
  - **First-Come, First-Served Basis**: Scholarships are strictly merit-based and allocated on a **first-come, first-served** basis according to quota availability in each slab.
  - Early application is strongly recommended before scholarship quotas for the academic year are exhausted.
