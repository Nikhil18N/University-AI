# SR University — Admissions, Eligibility, Comprehensive Fee Structure & Scholarships

## 1. Undergraduate (B.Tech) Admissions & General Eligibility
* **General Eligibility**: Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech programs at SR University must have passed 10+2 / Intermediate or an equivalent qualifying examination with a minimum of **60% aggregate marks** in required subjects (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics - PCM).
* **Mark Calculation & Total Marks**:
  - The standard total maximum marks for 12th class / Intermediate is **1000 marks** (in state boards like Telangana State BIE / Andhra Pradesh BIE).
  - Minimum cutoff of 60% corresponds to **600 marks out of 1000**.
  - 60% or 61% is **fully eligible**.
  - Applicants with scores strictly below 60% (e.g., 599 marks out of 1000 = 59.9%) fall below the cutoff and must contact admissions for special assessment.
* **Direct & Management Quota**:
  - No entrance exam (JEE Main, TS EAMCET, etc.) is required for direct admission. Admission is granted based on 10+2 PCM marks and seat availability.
* **Admissions Contact**:
  - Helpline: **833 100 3030** / **833 100 4040**
  - Email: `admissions@sru.edu.in`
  - Portal: `https://sru.edu.in/admissions`

## 6. Special Scholarship Categories
* **Special Category (Orphan / Deceased Parent)**:
  - Award: **15% additional scholarship** on tuition fee apart from eligible merit scholarship.
  - Required Documents: Death Certificate(s) of parent(s).
  - Continuation Criteria: Maintain minimum **7.5 CGPA** at the end of each semester without any backlogs or disciplinary cases.
