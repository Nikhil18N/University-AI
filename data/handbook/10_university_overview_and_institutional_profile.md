## 1. Vision & Campus Environment

A campus where every step connects with nature and every breath brings a spark of inspiration.

* **Campus Philosophy**: **SR University – A Next-Generation Living & Learning Campus**.
* **Green Campus & Scale**: Stands as the largest lush green campus for the next generation of innovators in Telangana, spread across 150 acres of environmentally conscious, green infrastructure.
* **Student Body**: 10,000+ active students representing states across India and diverse international nationalities.
* **Faculty Excellence**: World-class faculty holding doctoral (Ph.D.) and postdoctoral qualifications from prestigious institutions in India and abroad, including IITs, NITs, and premier international universities.

---

## 2. National & Global Accreditations & Rankings

India’s youngest and only private university in Telangana to achieve top national and global ranks:

* **NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework)**:
  - Ranked **91st in India** in the Engineering category (NIRF).
  - Maintained top 100 ranks consistently for four consecutive years.
* **NBA (National Board of Accreditation)**:
  - All B.Tech programs hold prestigious **Tier 1 accreditation** from the National Board of Accreditation.
* **ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)**:
  - The School of Agriculture received official **ICAR accreditation**, making SR University the **first private university in the Telugu States** (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh) to receive ICAR accreditation.
* **QS Asia University Rankings 2027**:
  - Globally, SR University ranks **19th among India's State Private Universities** in QS Asia 2027.
* **Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2027**:
  - Ranked **7th in India** in THE World University Rankings 2027.
  - Ranked **6th in India in Research Quality**.

## 3. Academic Programs & Innovation Ecosystem

SR University offers comprehensive multidisciplinary programmes designed for diverse career pathways:

* **Schools & Disciplines**:
  - School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
  - School of Engineering
  - School of Agriculture (ICAR Accredited)
  - School of Business (Astra School of Business)
  - School of Sciences, Design, and Emerging Disciplines
* **Academic Infrastructure**:
  - Modern digital classrooms with multimedia presentation tools.
  - High-tech laboratories and University Library.
  - Industry-grade facilities and setups for Artificial Intelligence (AI), Robotics, and emerging technologies.
  - Innovation hubs, incubation spaces, and project studios supporting student prototypes, real-world experiments, and engineering solutions.
* **Entrepreneurship & Startup Support (SRiX)**:
  - Through **SR Innovation Exchange (SRiX)**, students explore entrepreneurship, tech innovation, venture acceleration, and startup development.
* **Global Collaborations**:
  - **80+ international academic collaborations** providing students with global learning opportunities, joint research, exchange programs, and international exposure.

## 6. Living & Learning: Campus Hostels (Boys & Girls)

As the academic day comes to an end, the living and learning experience seamlessly continues in modern on-campus hostels:

* **Accommodation Options**: Dedicated, separate hostel blocks for Boys and Girls offering spacious **AC and Non-AC** room configurations.
* **Dining & Nutrition**:
  - **4,000-seating capacity central dining hall**.
  - Healthy, hygienic, and nutritious multi-cuisine meals served under strict quality standards.
* **Security & Safety**:
  - 24/7 round-the-clock physical security guards and complete electronic CCTV surveillance.
* **Room & Block Amenities**:
  - Well-furnished rooms with study desks, ergonomic chairs, and individual wardrobes.
  - High-speed 24x7 Wi-Fi connectivity across all hostel blocks and living areas.
  - Dedicated silent study areas, reading halls, and recreational/sports spaces.
* **Health & Emergency Care**:
  - On-campus Health and Wellness Centre with round-the-clock medical support and 24/7 emergency ambulance care.
