## 1. University identity (from sru.edu.in/about)

- SR University was notified in **May 2020** and is one of the first private universities in Telangana. It is recognised under **Section 2(f) of the UGC Act**.
- The university grew out of **SR Engineering College**, Warangal, which received autonomous status in 2014.
- **Sponsoring body:** Sri Rajeshwara Educational Society (SR Educational Academy), serving education since **1976**. The website describes the group as having 185 institutions across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka, more than 1 lakh students and 17,000 teaching and non-teaching staff.
- **Who owns or runs SR University?** It is a private university sponsored by Sri Rajeshwara Educational Society. The founder of the society, **Sri A. Varada Reddy**, is the **Chancellor**.
- **ARIIA 2020:** ranked 1st among private institutions.
- **SRiX** (SR Innovation Exchange), a Technology Business Incubator supported by DST, was inaugurated in March 2018.
- **Campus:** 170 acres at Ananthasagar, Hasanparthy, Warangal, Telangana 506371. **Corporate office:** 604, 6th Floor, Sonthalia Emerald, Raj Bhavan Road, Somajiguda, Hyderabad 500082.

## 3. Schools (from sru.edu.in/about)

Astra School of Business; School of Agriculture; School of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence; School of Design; School of Engineering; School of Sciences and Humanities. Levels offered: undergraduate, postgraduate, Ph.D., postdoctoral fellowship, D.Sc., and minors & honours.

## 4. Rankings and accreditations (website and 2027-28 fee card agree)

- **NIRF 2025:** 91st in the Engineering category (in the top 100 every year since 2021); 101-150 band in the University category.
- **NBA:** Tier-I accreditation for B.Tech programmes.
- **ICAR:** first private university in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh with ICAR accreditation (School of Agriculture).
- **Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027:** 801-1000 band; 7th among private universities in India.
- **QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027:** 701-710 band; 19th among private universities in India.

## 5. How to apply (from sru.edu.in/admission)

1. **Register online** on the admission portal (https://admission.sru.edu.in or https://apply.sru.edu.in).
2. **Complete the application:** enter details, choose a programme, upload documents.
3. **Submit scores:** JEE Main or EAPCET result where available; Intermediate / 10+2 marks are used for merit scholarships.
4. **Confirm the seat:** accept the offer and pay the fee.

The website's admission page does **not** list the documents to upload, the important dates or the refund policy. For these, the student must contact the Admissions Office (0870-2818333 / 0870-2818311, info@sru.edu.in).
