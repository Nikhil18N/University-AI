# Product Requirements Document

## Product
University AI Companion

## Problem
Students need one conversational interface for both everyday interaction and university
information. A traditional FAQ/PDF chatbot is too narrow because students also ask
greetings, follow-up questions, casual questions, and context-dependent questions.

## Goal
Build a friendly conversational AI companion that can:
1. Hold general conversations.
2. Answer university questions using trusted university sources.
3. Maintain conversation context.
4. Provide citations/source references for grounded university answers.
5. Support multilingual interaction.
6. Optionally integrate authenticated university services.

## Target users
- Students
- Faculty/staff (optional)
- University administrators

## MVP
- Chat UI
- Authentication
- General conversation
- University RAG
- Conversation history
- Source citations
- Feedback
- Admin document ingestion

## Future
- Student-specific APIs
- Voice
- Notifications
- Timetable/attendance/results
- Admin analytics
- Multilingual expansion

## Out of scope for MVP
- Autonomous academic decisions
- Unverified policy interpretation
- Actions such as fee payment without approved integrations
- Training a foundation model from scratch
