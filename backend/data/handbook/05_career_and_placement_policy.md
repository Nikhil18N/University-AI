## 3. Placement Eligibility Criteria
Students must satisfy all of the following requirements to register with CJAC and attend campus placement drives:
1. **Academic Standing**:
   - For general campus placements: Successfully completed all semester examinations with a **minimum CGPA of 6.0 or higher**, and **no active backlogs**.
   - For **Top Recruiters Placement Drives**: Must maintain a **minimum CGPA of 7.5 and above** with **no active backlogs**.
2. **Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) Attendance**:
   - Minimum **90% attendance** in all university-mandated job-ready training programs, mock interviews, coding assessments, and soft skills workshops.
3. **Financial & Conduct Standing**:
   - All university tuition and hostel fees must be cleared in full.
   - Satisfactory conduct record with no disciplinary actions or pending malpractice enquiries.
4. **Registration**:
   - Students must register their profile and validated academic records through the CJAC portal with verified contact details, resume, PAN card, and passport.

## 4. Placement Regulations & Selection Rules
* **Maximum Selection Attempts**:
  - A student is permitted a **maximum of five (5) selection process attempts** (reaching interview/final rounds).
  - If a student fails to secure a confirmed job offer after participating in the selection process of 5 organizations, they are automatically opted out of placement assistance unless exceptional permission is granted by management.
* **Job Acceptance Policy ('One Student – One Job')**:
  - The university strictly enforces a **"One Student – One Job"** policy. Once a student receives a confirmed offer letter from a company, they are not allowed to participate in further placement drives.
  - **Exception ($2\times$ CTC Rule)**: A student placed at a package of 'X' is permitted to participate in the recruitment drive of one additional "Dream/Super Dream" company that offers a CTC package of at least **$2\text{X}$** (double the current offer).
  - **Core Branch Exception**: Students from non-CSE core branches (Civil, Mechanical, EEE, ECE) who secure a placement in an IT/software company are permitted to appear for campus placement drives conducted by **Core Companies** in their respective branch of engineering.
  - **Non-Technical Profile Exception**: Students placed in non-technical roles (marketing, content development, business development) are permitted to appear in subsequent drives for technical profiles.

---

## 5. Early Joining & Internship Guidelines
* If an employer issues an offer letter requiring early joining during the 8th semester, the student must provide an official undertaking to complete all remaining coursework, semester exams, and capstone projects.
* The student must manage leaves from the company to write all university end-semester examinations.
* Early joining is permitted solely on the merits of the case upon written recommendation of the Department Head and Dean (Academics).

---

## 6. Debarment and Blacklisting Grounds
A student will be debarred / blacklisted from campus placement assistance in any of the following circumstances:
1. Involvement in any disciplinary violation or academic malpractice.
2. Furnishing false, exaggerated, or forged academic data/percentages to the placement cell.
3. Dropping out of a recruitment process once shortlisted after the online test/aptitude round.
4. Unprofessional behavior, misconduct, or rudeness reported by visiting HR representatives, company interviewers, or student volunteers.
5. Directly contacting visiting HR executives or recruiters without official CJAC authorization.
6. **Failure to Apply**: If a student is eligible as per the job description and company criteria but fails to apply for **three (3) consecutive eligible campus job postings**, they will be deemed disinterested and disqualified from the placement process.
