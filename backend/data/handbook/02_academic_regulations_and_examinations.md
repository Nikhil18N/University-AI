# SR University — Academic Regulations & Examination Rules (R22 & R23)

## 1. Scope and Applicable Batches
These Academic Regulations govern all undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs for the **2022–23 and 2023–24 academic batches** and onwards at SR University.
* **Medium of Instruction & Examination**: English.
* **Academic Council**: Principal academic authority as per UGC regulations for instruction, education, examinations, and research.

## 3. Semester Structure & Working Days
* **Odd Semester**: July/August to November/December.
* **Even Semester**: December/January to April/May.
* **Instructional Days**:
  - Engineering, Business, Sciences: 14 to 18 weeks (minimum **90 working days** including examinations).
  - School of Agricultural Sciences: Minimum **110 working days** including examinations.
* **Summer / Winter Term**: An optional fast-track term offered during vacations to clear backlogs, improve grades, or earn additional credits for Honours/Minors.

## 4. Attendance Policy & Condonation Rules
* **Standard Requirement**: A student is expected to maintain **100% attendance** in all courses.
* **Minimum Permitted Attendance**: A student must maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance** in each course without producing any proof of absence. The 25% allowed absence covers medical leave, casual absence, family emergencies, official event participation, technical/cultural fests, NSS/NCC camps, and sports.
* **Shortage of Attendance (Condonation)**:
  - If attendance falls marginally below 75% due to severe medical reasons or other valid grounds, the case may be recommended by the School Dean with documentary evidence to the Dean (Academics).
  - The **Condonation Board** led by Dean (Academics) may grant a **10% to 25% waiver** at its discretion.
  - A financial penalty (condonation fee) will be levied as prescribed in Annexure-I.
  - **School of Agriculture Exception**: For students of the School of Agriculture, **only up to a 10% waiver** (i.e. minimum 65% attendance) is permitted under condonation board discretion.
* **Detention in a Course**:
  - A student whose attendance in a course is below 75% (or below condoned threshold) is **detained** in that course.
  - The student is awarded an **"R" grade** (Inadequate Attendance / Detained) and will NOT be permitted to sit for the End Semester Examination.
  - Such students must re-register for the course during the summer or winter semester by paying the required fee.

## 5. Grading System & Calculation Formulas
Evaluation is based on Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and End Semester Examinations (ESE). Letter grades and grade points are awarded on a 10-point scale:

### Letter Grades (Engineering, Management, Sciences, BCA, MCA)
| Letter Grade | Performance | Grade Point | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **O** | Outstanding | **10** | Awarded to exceptional performers among 'A' grade students |
| **A** | Excellent | **10** | Pass grade |
| **B** | Very Good | **8** | Pass grade |
| **C** | Good | **6** | Pass grade |
| **D** | Average | **4** | Minimum Pass Grade (40% aggregate for UG, 50% for PG) |
| **R** | Inadequate Attendance / Detained / Dropped | **0** | Must re-register in Summer/Winter semester |
| **F** | Fail | **0** | Backlog course; must appear in supplementary or re-register |
| **I** | Incomplete (Medical reasons for ESE) | **0** | Re-exam permitted in supplementary |

### Equivalent Grade Chart (R20/R21 vs R22/R23)
* R20/R21 Grade Points: `O: 10`, `A+: 9`, `A: 8`, `B+: 7`, `B: 6`, `C: 5`, `F: 0`.
* R22/R23 Grade Points: `O: 10`, `A: 10`, `B: 8`, `C: 6`, `D: 4`, `F: 0`.

### Formulas
* **SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)**:
  $$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}$$
  where $C_i$ is course credits and $G_i$ is grade points earned in that semester.
* **CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)**:
  $$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}$$
  computed across all completed semesters. 'I' grades are excluded until cleared.
* **Percentage Conversion Formula**:
  $$\text{Percentage of Marks} = 10 \times \text{CGPA}$$

## 6. Academic Progression & Promotion Rules
To be promoted to subsequent academic years in B.Tech programs, a student must meet the following minimum requirements:
* **Promotion to Second Year**:
  - Secure a **CGPA $\ge 3.50$** OR earn a minimum of **40% of the credits** offered in the first year curriculum.
* **Promotion to Third Year**:
  - Secure a **CGPA $\ge 4.00$** OR earn a minimum of **50% of the credits** offered in the first year curriculum.
* **Promotion to Fourth Year**:
  - Secure a **CGPA $\ge 4.00$** OR earn a minimum of **50% of the credits** offered in the second year curriculum.
* **Failure to Meet Promotion Criteria**: Students who fail to meet both criteria must repeat the entire academic year.

### School of Agriculture Promotion Rules
* **Promotion to 2nd Year**: Automatically promoted irrespective of failed courses.
* **Promotion to 3rd Year**: Must have passed **all 1st year courses compulsorily without any backlogs** (including non-gradial / elective courses) and attended 2nd year.
* **Promotion to 4th Year**: Must have passed **all 2nd year courses compulsorily without any backlogs** and attended 3rd year.
* **OGPA Division Classification for Agriculture**:
  - `OGPA 5.000 – 5.999`: Pass
  - `OGPA 6.000 – 6.999`: II Division
  - `OGPA 7.000 – 7.999`: I Division
  - `OGPA 8.000 and above`: I Division with Distinction

## 7. Backlogs, Make-up & Supplementary Examinations
* **F Grade Policy**:
  - Students with an 'F' grade may either register for the course again when offered, or register for the supplementary examination without attending classes.
  - Only **one supplementary chance** is allowed per course. If not cleared, the student must repeat the course during the winter/summer semester.
  - Maximum grade awarded in supplementary examination for an 'F' grade student is **C grade**.
* **I Grade (Medical Incomplete)**:
  - Supplementary exam is treated as a re-conduct of the end semester examination.
  - Marks secured in the supplementary exam substitute the ESE marks, and full grading scale applies.
* **Make-up Test for Mid-Term**:
  - If a student misses a mid-term exam or scores less than 50% due to genuine reasons, a make-up exam option is provided upon payment of makeup exam fee.

## 8. Honours and Minor Degrees
* **Degree with Honours**:
  - Eligibility: Must maintain a **CGPA of 7.0 or higher** before starting honours courses and maintain $\ge 7.0$ at graduation.
  - Requirements: Earn a minimum of **16 additional credits** through advanced courses via overloading during regular or summer/winter semesters.
  - Fee: Extra fees payable for honours courses. Grades earned do not affect regular SGPA/CGPA.
* **Minor Degree**:
  - Eligibility: Must possess a **CGPA of 6.0 or higher** to register.
  - Requirements: Earn a minimum of **16 additional credits** by completing courses from another department.
  - Extra open electives can also count towards the minor degree.

---

## 9. Grade Improvement Policy
* **Eligibility**: Students with grades **B, C, or D** in any course are eligible for grade improvement.
* **Attempts**: Maximum of **one attempt** per course.
* **Capping**: There is **no capping of grades** — students can even achieve an **A or O grade**.
* **Evaluation**: Full coursework and evaluation components must be taken. The **better of the two grades** is retained.
* **Timing**: Must be registered before the summer semester. Not permitted after the student has graduated.

---

## 10. Change of Branch / Upgradation
* **Timing**: Conducted at the end of the 1st year (after completing the first two semesters).
* **Criteria**:
  1. Students in the **top 1% based on CGPA until 2nd semester** are permitted to change to any branch of their choice (subject to seat availability).
  2. Students with **CGPA $\ge 8.0$** and no backlogs are eligible to apply, subject to seat availability and university discretion.
* **Conditions**: Change of branch once granted is final and binding. All branch changes take effect from the 3rd semester. Students in International Engineering Programs are not eligible for branch upgradation.
