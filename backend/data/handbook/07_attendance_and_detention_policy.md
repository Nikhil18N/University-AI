# SR University — Attendance Regulations, Condonation & Detention Policy

## 1. Official Attendance Policy
* **Standard Requirement**: A student is expected to maintain **100% attendance** in all registered courses in every semester.
* **Minimum Prescribed Attendance**: A student must maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance** in each individual course.
* **Absence Allowance**: The 25% allowed absence is intended to accommodate casual leave, illness, minor personal emergencies, technical/cultural festivals, sports, and NSS/NCC camps without needing proof.
* **Myth Check — Is 50% attendance allowed?**: **NO**. The university does NOT allow 50% attendance for engineering or any other students. Any claim that students can apply for a "50% attendance relaxation" is completely false.

---

## 2. Shortage of Attendance & Medical Condonation
* **Marginal Shortage (Below 75%)**: If a student's attendance falls marginally below 75% due to severe medical exigencies (such as hospitalization, typhoid, dengue, surgery, or serious accident) or official university duty:
  - The student/parent must inform the department and submit official medical certificates and hospital discharge summaries.
  - The School Dean forwards genuine cases with adequate proof to the **Condonation Board** led by the **Dean (Academics)**.
* **Condonation Board Waiver**:
  - The Condonation Board has the discretion to grant a **10% to 25% attendance waiver** (allowing eligible students with attendance between **65% and 74%** to be condoned).
  - In such condoned cases, a financial penalty (condonation fee) is levied in proportion to the shortfall as specified in Annexure-I.
  - **School of Agriculture Exception**: For students of the School of Agriculture, **only up to a 10% waiver** (minimum 65% attendance) can be considered.
* **Hospitalization / Illness Hope (e.g. 71% attendance)**:
  - A student who has **71% attendance** because of hospitalization for severe typhoid is **within the 10%–25% condonation waiver window** (above 65%).
  - The student is **NOT automatically or permanently detained** if they submit their medical and hospitalization documentation to the School Dean and Dean (Academics) for Condonation Board review and pay the condonation fee upon approval.

---

## 3. Course Detention & Award of 'R' Grade
* **Detention Threshold**: Any student who fails to secure the minimum 75% attendance in a course and is not granted condonation by the Condonation Board **is deemed to be detained in that course**.
* **Grade Awarded**: Detained students are awarded an **"R" grade** (Inadequate Attendance / Detained).
* **Examination Consequence**: A student awarded an 'R' grade is **barred from appearing in the End Semester Examination (ESE)** for that course.
* **Clearing 'R' Grade**: An 'R' grade cannot be cleared through supplementary examinations. The student must re-register for the course during the winter or summer fast-track semester by paying the required course fee.
