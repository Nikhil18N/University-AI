# SR University: strengths, faculty and campus visits (checked 17 November 2026)

> Every line below was read on the official pages named in brackets on 17 November 2026.
> Fees and scholarships are NOT on this page: they come only from the 2027-28 fee card.

## 1. Why SR University: rankings and recognition

- NIRF 2025: 91st in the Engineering category (up from 98th); 101-150 band in the University category. [2027-28 fee card; sru.edu.in]
- THE World University Rankings 2027: 801-1000 band worldwide; 7th among private universities in India. [2027-28 fee card]
- QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027: 701-710 band; 19th among private universities in India. [2027-28 fee card; sru.edu.in news]
- ICAR: the first private university in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh to achieve ICAR accreditation (School of Agriculture). [2027-28 fee card]
- These ranks place SR University among the top-ranked private universities of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. When saying so, always give the rank behind it.
- UGC recognised under Section 2(f); established under the Telangana State Private Universities Act. [sru.edu.in]

## 2. Placements and careers [sru.edu.in/placements]

- Highest package: 51 LPA. Average package: 6.5 LPA. Top-10-percentile average: 21 LPA.
- 150+ recruiters, 25 industry partners, 1,300+ job offers.
- Recent offers named on the page include 44 LPA (ServiceNow), 45 LPA, 34.5 LPA, 25 LPA, 19.75 LPA, 16.75 LPA and 14.5 LPA for CSE students.
- Training: career counselling, aptitude and coding assessments, communication and leadership skills, internships and live projects, industry mentorship and alumni connect.
- Director of Placements: GuruCharn Singh (director.placements@sru.edu.in).
- The page gives two different placement-rate figures (92% and 95%): do not quote a placement percentage until the Placement Office confirms one. Placement is never guaranteed.

## 3. Industry connections and entrepreneurship [sru.edu.in/about, sru.edu.in/research]

- 171+ startups; the SRiX technology business incubator and the NEST centre for entrepreneurship in science and technology (12 startups incubating, 30+ graduated, Rs 1.2 crore+ funded student projects).
- NEST industry mentors include managers from Microsoft and Hindustan Unilever.
- Research centres: AI & Deep Learning; Embedded Systems & IoT; Emerging Energy Technologies; Materials & Manufacturing; Nanomaterials; Construction Methods & Materials; Design; Creative Cognition; Informetrics & Statistics; Experiential Learning; Collaboratory for Social Innovation.

## 4. International partnerships and opportunities [sru.edu.in/about, sru.edu.in/international]

- 80+ international partners.
- International Affairs office and a 2+2 study abroad programme (two years at SRU, two years at a partner university).
- International students from across Asia and Africa; scholarships for international students; visa, arrival and settling-in support; separate international food options in the hostels.
- Credit transfer options for mobility between universities.

## 5. Research [sru.edu.in home page and news]

- 9,300+ research papers in peer-reviewed international journals; 130+ top-10-percentile publications.
- 2,700+ patents filed (news article "2700 patents filed").

## 7. Campus visits [sru.edu.in/visit-us]

- SR University has one campus: Ananthasagar, Hasanparthy, Warangal Urban 506371, Telangana.
- Campus visits are arranged by the admissions team. SRU Buddy does not book or confirm visits.
- For a campus visit: 0870-2818333 / 0870-2818311, info@sru.edu.in, +91 70750 10336 (also on WhatsApp).
- Getting here: Warangal Railway Station and Kazipet Junction are the nearest major stations; Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Hyderabad is the nearest major airport (about 140 km), then by road or rail.
- The visit page does not list visiting hours: ask the admissions team for timings.
