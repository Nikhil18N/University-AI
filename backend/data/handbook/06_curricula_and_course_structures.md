# SR University — Curricula & Academic Course Structures (R23 & R22)

## 1. Curriculum Framework & Credit System
All undergraduate and postgraduate programs at SR University follow the **Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS)** under an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) model.
* **Credit Value Definition**:
  - 1 Credit = 1 Lecture / Tutorial hour per week during a semester.
  - 1 Credit = 2 Practical / Laboratory / Project hours per week during a semester.
* **B.Tech Course Classification**:
  - **HS**: Humanities and Social Sciences
  - **BS**: Basic Sciences (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  - **ES**: Engineering Sciences (Programming, Digital Logic, Mechanics)
  - **PC**: Program Core (Discipline-specific compulsory courses)
  - **PE**: Program Electives
  - **SC / SE**: Specialization Core & Specialization Electives
  - **OE**: Open Electives (Interdisciplinary courses available across all branches)
  - **HCE**: Hobbies, Co-Curricular and Extra-Curricular Electives (1 credit each: Sports, Club, NSS, Music, Dance, Hackathon, Photography, Fitness, Cooking)
  - **CRE**: Career Readiness Electives (Quantitative aptitude, verbal reasoning, communication)
  - **PR**: Projects, Internships, Capstone Design, Seminar

## 2. B.Tech Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence (CS&AI) — R23
* **Degree Requirements**: Total **160 Credits** over 8 semesters.
* **Semester-Wise Summary**: For example it way vary by subjects and semesters.
  - **Semester I (22 Credits)**: Language Skills (`23EN301HS102`), Smart Product Design (`23EC102ES101`), Problem Solving Using Programming Elective-I (`23CS303ES101`, Python/C/Java), Basic Science Elective-I, Mathematics Elective-I.
  - **Semester II (22 Credits)**: Discrete Mathematical Structures (`23CS310BS103`), Digital Logic (`23EC301ES103`), Environment and Sustainability (`23CH300BS102`), Life Skills (`23EN100HS101`), Problem Solving Elective-II, Mathematics Elective-II, Entrepreneurship & Startup (`23BM100HS101`), HCE-I.
  - **Semester III (23 Credits)**: Data Structures (`23CS313PC203`, 7 credits), Operating Systems (`23CS301PC204`), Computer Networks (`23CS311PC205`), Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (`23CS301PC206`), Specialization Core-I.
  - **Semester IV (25 Credits)**: Design & Analysis of Algorithms (`23CS302PC209`), Microprocessors & Computer Architecture (`23CS301PC207`), Database Management Systems (`23CS301PC208`), Seminar in Advanced Technologies (`23CS010PR201`), Specialization Core-II, Math Elective-III, Career Readiness-I, LHS-I.
  - **Semester V (23 Credits)**: Theory of Computation & Formal Methods (`23CS310PC301`), Software Engineering (`23CS310PC302`), Undergraduate Research (`23CS010PC303`), Program Elective-I, Specialization Elective-I, Career Readiness-II, Open Elective-I, Intro to Cloud Computing (`23CS101PC304`).
  - **Semester VI (13 Credits)**: Program Elective-II, Specialization Elective-II, Open Elective-II, Open Elective-III, LHS-III.
  - **Semester VII (15 Credits)**: Capstone Project (`23CS009PR401`, 9 credits), Program Elective-III, Open Elective-IV (OR full-semester Industry Internship/Startup: `23CS015PR402`, 15 credits).
  - **Semester VIII (15 Credits)**: Major Project / Industry Internship / Startup Externship (`23CS015PR403`, 15 credits).

## 7. Management, Computer Applications & Agricultural Programs

  - Specializations: Marketing, Finance, HR, Business Analytics.
* **BCA R23 (120 Credits, 6 Semesters)**:

  - **MBA R23**: Dual specializations across Marketing, Finance, HR, Business Analytics, Supply Chain.
  - **MCA R23**: Advanced Data Structures, DBMS, Software Engineering, AI, Cloud Computing, Full-Semester Major Project.
